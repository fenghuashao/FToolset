# FToolset 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

适用版本 / Version 1.0.0

文档日期 / Document date 2026-11-22

## 简体中文

FToolset 完全在本机运行，安装后可离线使用。开发者不收集任何用户数据，扩展也不传输任何用户数据。扩展无需账号，且没有广告、遥测或使用统计。

- **生成二维码。** 打开扩展时，若 Chrome 授权读取当前活动标签页的 URL，FToolset 用它填充内容框。你也可以输入链接或文字，选择要生成二维码的网页文字或链接，以及选用本地图片作为中心图标。内容和图标在浏览器内处理，用于生成、预览、复制和下载二维码。
- **本地存储。** FToolset 在当前 Chrome 配置中记住上次使用的工具。右键菜单提交的所选文字或链接只在浏览器会话内等待弹窗读取，读取后移除。未读取的数据会随浏览器重启或扩展禁用、重载而清除。手动输入、图标和二维码结果留在当前弹窗，关闭后不保留在扩展存储。扩展不记录网页访问历史，也不使用 Chrome 同步存储。
- **复制与下载。** 点击复制时，扩展把生成的 PNG 写入系统剪贴板。点击下载时，Chrome 将文件保存到本机。复制或下载的副本由你管理。卸载 FToolset 会清除扩展自己的本地设置。

FToolset 仅为上述用户主动发起的功能使用这些数据，遵守 Chrome Web Store 用户数据政策及其 Limited Use 要求。

## English

FToolset runs entirely on your device and works offline after installation. The developer does not collect any user data, and the extension does not transmit any user data. It requires no account and contains no ads, telemetry, or usage analytics.

- **QR generation.** When you open FToolset, Chrome may grant temporary access to the active tab's URL. FToolset uses it to fill the content field. You can also enter a link or text, select page text or a link from the context menu, and choose a local image as a center logo. Chrome processes this content and image on your device to generate, preview, copy, or download the QR code.
- **Local storage.** FToolset remembers your last tool in this Chrome profile. Text or a link from the context menu waits in the browser session and is removed when the popup reads it. An unread item is cleared when Chrome restarts or when the extension is disabled or reloaded. Manual input, logos, and QR results stay in the open popup. They are not kept in extension storage after you close it. FToolset keeps no browsing history and uses no Chrome sync storage.
- **Copy and download.** When you choose Copy PNG, FToolset writes the generated image to your system clipboard. When you choose Download, Chrome saves the file on your device. You control these copies. Uninstalling FToolset removes its local extension settings.

FToolset uses data only for the functions described above that you choose to use. It complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
